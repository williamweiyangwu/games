EUROPE 1939Choose your nation · 50 gold in the treasury · Conquer the continent

Nations that fought in the Second World War are on the map. You begin with 50 gold, three riflemen and two mine fields at home.
There is no daily income — mine fields are the only gold in the game, 4 gold a day each. Buy mine cars to lay more, and take countries to grow your yield.
Click a country to open its sheet: a small map of the country with the units standing in it. Recruit in your own territory, then order units into a neighbouring country. Every front is a real front — the AI nations fight each other too, so a war can be won without fighting the whole continent.
Units fight at their best on friendly soil, and hardest of all in their homeland. Tanks, artillery and bombers cost upkeep every day, so a huge army can bankrupt you.
You win by holding every territory. You lose if you hold none.
