EUROPE 1939Choose your nation · your own treasury and army · Conquer the continent

Nations that fought in the Second World War are on the map. Each begins with its own treasury, riflemen and two mine fields at home — a major power far more than a micro-state.
There is no daily income — mine fields are the only gold in the game, mined at each nation's own rate. Buy mine cars to lay more, and take countries to grow your yield.
Click a country to open its sheet: a small map of the country with the units standing in it. Recruit in your own territory, then order units into a neighbouring country. Every front is a real front — the AI nations fight each other too, so a war can be won without fighting the whole continent.
Units fight at their best on friendly soil, and hardest of all in their homeland. Tanks, artillery and bombers cost upkeep every day, so a huge army can bankrupt you.
Army sizes follow real history: micro-states like Albania or Denmark hold just 2–3 units, while a major power fields up to 14. The Soviet Union is split into three fronts — South 4, Centre 4, North 5 — and a weak state buys only basic rifles, guns and fighters: no tanks, heavy armour or bomber fleets.
Send your men at a front and they hold the ground until the country falls — only then do the survivors march back home to rearm. Only one territory on the whole map changes hands each day.
You win by holding every territory. You lose if you hold none.

🎓 HOW TO PLAY
1

Pick a country

Aim of the war: hold every territory on the map. You start with your nation's own treasury and riflemen and two mine fields at home. The Soviet Union is split into three fronts of its own — South, Centre and North.

2

Money comes from mines

There is no daily income — mine fields pay 4 gold a day each. Buy mine cars in the Shop and send them to your front to lay more fields. No mines, no money to pay for troops.

3

Recruit & move

Click a territory you own to open it, pick a unit in the Shop. Then click Battle, tick your units, and click a flashing border to send them into a neighbouring country. Select every unit on my fronts grabs your whole line at once. They hold the ground until it falls, then the survivors retire home.

4

One piece a day

Only one territory on the whole map can change hands each day — whoever has held the front longest takes it. Attack on several borders and the strongest claim wins. When a nation loses its last piece it capitulates and its army disbands.

5

Defend your homeland

Units fight at their best on friendly soil, and hardest of all in their home country. Mines maul attackers. Tanks, artillery and bombers cost upkeep every day, so a huge army can bankrupt you.

6

Speed controls

Use 0.5×, 1×, 2× or 3× to speed up or slow the war down, and Pause to stop it. Open News for dispatches and Mail to talk to other nations.
