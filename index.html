SPORTS

WHISTLE

3D first-person referee simulator. You are in his boots, whistle in hand — take charge of twelve real World Cup and Champions League classics, or draw a sixteen-team World Cup or an eight-team Champions League knockout at random. Nothing starts without your whistle: kick-offs, corners, goal kicks, free kicks and penalties all wait for you. Decide goal or no goal, send it to VAR, describe every foul and choose play on, a warning, yellow or red, then spray the free-kick mark and place the wall exactly where you want it. Sharper keepers and strikers make every shot count. Points, kits and trophies can be backed up as a code, so wiping your browser does not cost you your career.
