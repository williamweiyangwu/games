RACING TUTORIAL

CONTROLS:
W / ↑ — Throttle  |  S / ↓ — Brake/Reverse
A/D / ←/→ — Steering  |  SHIFT — Handbrake (Drift)
SPACE — Nitro Boost  |  C — Camera  |  R — Restart
M — Mute  |  ESC — Pause  |  Touch buttons on mobile

RACING LINE (green line): Shows the ideal path. Enter wide, hit the apex (inside of the turn), exit wide for maximum speed.

WHEN TO DO WHAT:
• Straights: Full throttle. Use Nitro (SPACE) when clear ahead.
• Before corners: Brake early (S/↓), down to a controllable speed.
• Turn-in: Ease off brake, steer smoothly toward the apex.
• Mid-corner: Maintain steady throttle/steering; avoid sudden inputs.
• Corner exit: Unwind steering and accelerate progressively.
• Tight hairpins: Brake harder, consider a small drift with SHIFT for rotation, then power out.

RACE START: 5 red lights illuminate, then all go out — accelerate on lights out. Accelerating early = jump start (+10s penalty).

PIT STOPS: Near the start/finish straight, move to the inside edge and slow down to trigger service (repairs damage, refuels, fresh tyres).

TRACK NUMBER (1-150): In the menu, enter any track number from 1–150 and click LOAD to race that track (tracks beyond the presets are procedurally generated).
