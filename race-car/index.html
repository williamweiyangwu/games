RACING TUTORIAL

CONTROLS:
W / ↑ — Throttle  |  S / ↓ — Brake/Reverse
A/D / ←/→ — Steering  |  SHIFT — Handbrake Drift
SPACE — Nitro Boost  |  C — Camera  |  R — Restart
M — Mute  |  ESC — Pause  |  Touch buttons on mobile

RACING LINE (green arrows): Shows the ideal path. Enter wide, hit the apex (inside of the turn), exit wide for maximum speed.
RED BRAKE BOARDS: The three red bars before each corner are the braking zone. Brake at the first bar, then release as you turn in.

WHEN TO DO WHAT:
• Straights: Full throttle. Use Nitro (SPACE) when clear ahead.
• Before corners: Brake at the red boards on the racing line (S/↓), down to a controllable speed.
• Turn-in: Ease off brake, steer smoothly toward the apex.
• Mid-corner: Maintain steady throttle/steering; avoid sudden inputs.
• Corner exit: Unwind steering and accelerate progressively.
• Tight hairpins: Brake harder, consider a small drift with SHIFT for rotation, then power out.

NITRO: You start at 35% and recharge 2% every second. Drifting does not charge it — save SPACE for the straights.

RACE START: 5 red lights illuminate, then all go out — accelerate on lights out. Accelerating early = jump start (+10s penalty).

PIT STOPS: Follow the yellow chevrons and the big PIT ENTRY gantry on the approach, then press P. The car obeys the 80 km/h pit limit, drives to its box and stops for a real ~2.6 s tyre change (+1.2 s with repairs). Choose your compound in the menu. The AI pits too when tyres wear or damage builds.

LAPS ROLLER: Drag the LAPS slider in the menu to set the race length from 1 to 50 laps. Pick your real F1 circuit from the TRACK cards above.
