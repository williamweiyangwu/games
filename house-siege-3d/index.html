HOUSE SIEGE 3D

5v5 — TEAM DEATHMATCH & CAPTURE THE FLAG

GAME MODE

AI DIFFICULTY

FIELD MANUAL

OBJECTIVE

  • Team Deathmatch: You are on the BLUE team. Out-kill the RED team before the 5-minute timer ends. 3 second respawn.
  • Capture the Flag: Steal the RED flag and bring it to YOUR HQ pad. Each return scores a point. Most points in 5 minutes wins.

CONTROLS

  • WASD — Move   Mouse — Aim   Left Click — Attack
  • 1–6 — Switch weapon   R — Reload   E — Open chest / interact
  • Shift — Sprint   Space — Jump   ESC — Pause

LOADOUT & CHESTS

You start with only a KNIFE and a PISTOL. Weapon crates glow around the house — walk up and press E to grab a random weapon (Assault Rifle, Shotgun, Sniper, Rocket Launcher) with ammo. Crates restock after 20 seconds.

AI DIFFICULTY

  • EASY — Slow reaction, poor accuracy, 80 HP.
  • NORMAL — Average soldier, 100 HP.
  • HARD — Fast reactions, good accuracy, 120 HP.
  • LEGEND — Near-perfect aim, deadly fast, 140 HP.

HEALTH BARS

Bars above heads: GREEN = teammate, RED = enemy. Your own HP is bottom-left.
