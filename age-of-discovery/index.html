AGE OF DISCOVERY

~ A Life in Lisbon ~
Lisbon, the year of our Lord 1498. You are a common citizen — no ship, no crew, scarcely a coin to your name. You have one hour of life before you. Haul cargo on the docks, commission a ship at the estaleiro, hire brave sailors in the tavern, and sail beyond the edge of the known world. Fish off the pier head, pray at the Sé, and talk with every citizen you meet — each one will trade words with you, and most will throw dice or ask riddles for silver. Buy where goods are cheap, sell them at home, and carve your name into the age — from unknown citizen to Fidalgo of the Realm. When your hour is done, the ledger of your life will be read aloud. But beware: the sea is vast, and a man can die an unnatural death upon it.
